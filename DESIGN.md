# Design decisions

The design documents now live in [`docs/`](docs/README.md), in the Builder OS format:

- **[docs/design.md](docs/design.md):** the one rule (one screen, one job), the four layouts, the component kit, every small interaction and why it exists, and screen-by-screen notes.
- **[docs/brand-guide.md](docs/brand-guide.md):** colours, type, motion, voice, and what the brand is not. Rendered at [/brand-guide.html](https://architect-2-0-alpha.vercel.app/brand-guide.html).
- **[docs/prd.md](docs/prd.md):** the problem, the three people, and how we'll know it worked.
- **[docs/journeys.md](docs/journeys.md):** every flow and all 22 screens with their states.

Short version: **building an agent app is no longer the hard part; getting people to trust it is.** So Architect 2.0 is built for three people, the builder, the expert and IT, and every screen does one job.
